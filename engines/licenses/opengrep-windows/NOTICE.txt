opengrep Windows DLL notices

The Windows wheel ships opengrep.exe and the MinGW-w64 DLLs from
opengrep_windows_x86.zip. The DLLs come unchanged from Cygwin packages.

inventory.json links the Cygwin source packages (upstream tarballs, patches
and build scripts) and identifies the upstream notice files and source
ranges. Only trailing whitespace and final newlines may differ. GMP's source
package is also kept at
https://github.com/nikitatsym/tackbox/releases/download/third-party-sources/mingw64-x86_64-gmp-6.3.0-1-src.tar.zst

opengrep.exe and the DLLs include parts of the MinGW-w64 runtime; see
mingw-w64-runtime.COPYING.txt.
