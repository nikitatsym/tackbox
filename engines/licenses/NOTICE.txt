Bundled engine dependency licenses

The javalint/ and go/ directories contain pinned dependency inventories
and verbatim upstream license/notice texts for the Java and Go engines.
Their contents ship in thin wheels under tackbox/third_party/licenses;
javalint/ also ships inside javalint.jar under META-INF/third-party.

Each engine directory's NOTICE.txt identifies the applicable license terms
and acknowledgments. Component versions, source URLs, and license paths are
owned by that directory's inventory.json.

Dependency upgrades must update the affected inventory entries and any
changed license or notice texts. The Java inventory also records original
jar hashes. Maven runtime dependencies come from java/pom.xml; linked Go
modules come from `go list -deps` for the three bundled commands.
